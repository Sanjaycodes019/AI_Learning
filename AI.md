# Artificial Intelligence (AI)

## What is AI?
- AI is the broad field of designing systems that solve problems using intelligence-like capabilities.
- It includes perception, reasoning, learning, planning, and decision-making.
- AI systems can range from simple rule-based programs to advanced learning systems.

## Key ideas
- Intelligence is not a single algorithm.
- AI can be symbolic (rules and logic) or learning-based.
- Real AI applications often combine multiple methods.

## Example concept
- A simple AI rule-based system can choose actions based on conditions:

```python
user_input = "weather"
if "weather" in user_input:
    response = "I can tell you the weather forecast."
elif "news" in user_input:
    response = "I can show you today's headlines."
else:
    response = "I can help with weather and news."
print(response)
```

This example shows how an AI-like program can process input and choose a response.
